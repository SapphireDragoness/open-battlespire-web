OpenBattlespire

An open-source engine for An Elder Scrolls Legend: Battlespire (1997). It plays the game from your own copy: choose the folder where Battlespire is installed (the GOG or Steam release).

The files are read here, in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
